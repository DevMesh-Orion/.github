# Code of Conduct

DevMesh-Orion is intended to be a welcoming and constructive technical community.

## Expected Behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully
- focus on technical issues rather than individuals
- provide constructive feedback
- respect different levels of experience
- avoid harassment or discriminatory behavior
- avoid sharing private or sensitive information
- follow the rules of the relevant platform

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment
- threats
- discrimination
- targeted abuse
- doxxing
- publishing private information
- malicious behavior
- deliberately disrupting projects or services

## Moderation

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community, project, or its users.

Serious incidents may be handled privately.

## Reporting

Community-related concerns should be reported privately to the project maintainers whenever possible.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported according to `SECURITY.md`.
